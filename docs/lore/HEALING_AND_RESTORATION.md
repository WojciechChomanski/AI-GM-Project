# Healing and restoration in the world (lore/rules note)

- **Humans** heal with the medicine of the age: bandages, poultices, the surgeon's saw and the cleric's herbs. A male
  cleric is a priest-physician, not a miracle worker. No man wields active magic (core_rules.txt).
- **Dwarves** heal faster than any son of Adam (1.5x) as long as they eat. Their master smiths forge rune-bound
  prostheses of iron, bronze, star-bronze or adamantine that work like the lost limb or better, sometimes with a hidden
  blade, bolt thrower or hand gun inside. They are sold to men and even ogres who can pay, never to elves.
- **Elves** regrow what war takes, through alchemy, rituals and magic stones, at a high price, but never for a dwarf.
  This is why elves look untouched by years and battle: they keep no scars.
- **Veil healing** by a sorceress works, and the Veil always takes its price.
- **Saint relics** are the only human way to restore a lost limb.
