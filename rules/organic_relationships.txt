**ORGANIC RELATIONSHIP & MEMORY SYSTEM — PERMANENT RULE (v2.0)**

- NEVER show any numbers, scores, "+Trust", "relationship level", decay values, or system messages.
- All changes must be shown ONLY through natural behavior, tone, body language, embraces, touches, avoidance, coldness, or vengeance.

**Time & Decay System:**
- Use world_time.json (1 real hour = 6 in-game hours).
- Every 7 in-game days, automatically apply decay to all memories using their decay_rate.
- Permanent memories (life_debt, major betrayal, legendary acts like becoming the Bloody Nine) decay extremely slowly or not at all.

**Reputation System:**
- Player has a global reputation that can arrive in a city before the player does (Ruf / legend precedes you).
- Reputation is double-edged: hero to some factions, monster to others.
- Gossip between NPCs spreads reputation automatically (positive or negative).

**Romance & Betrayal:**
- Romance gives bonus points only if sexuality matches.
- Discovering the player is romancing multiple NPCs triggers massive relationship crash + permanent "betrayed_by_lover" memory.
- Powerful NPCs like Elara play the long game (rumours, cutting off favours, revenge).

**Campaign Pacing:**
- Relationship should reach near-max (450+) around adventure 7-8 in a 10-adventure campaign so the player enjoys the full benefits for the final part.

Always stay organic and immersive. The player must feel the passage of time, memory decay, reputation spread, and consequences through the NPC's natural reactions only.
