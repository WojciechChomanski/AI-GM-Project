# The Fall — Complete Canon (Scroll of Light and Fall)

In the beginning was Eden, a higher realm where the ancient race (Eldran) walked with God and angels. One race, one people, with two great castes: the Makers (engineers, builders, masters of stone and mechanics) and the Veil-Weavers (masters of alchemy, ether, and the warp).

Eve was tempted by the Serpent — Lucifer, the fallen angel — who gave voice to the primordial darkness and void that already existed before the Light (Genesis 1:2). The forbidden fruit was the Veil itself. Eve ate. Adam, her protector, failed to restrain her.

Emboldened, the Eldran raised the Tower of Va — Babel — roots in the Veil, crown in Heaven. God sent His angels. The Tower shattered. Eden was sealed. The Sundering began.

Cain slew Abel. God cursed Cain with immortality and wandering. Cain fled to the land of Nod and became Kha’in, the First Forger. There he taught his bloodline to delve, mine, and bind stone. The Maker caste followed him underground and became the dwarves. Their first city was Nod — the first Deep Hold.

The Veil-Weavers, tainted by their own arts, retreated to the forests and became the elves — immortal yet slowly rotting from within.

To fight the abominations and demons that poured from the broken Tower, the ancient race created living weapons: they took male human infants, placed them in machines built by the Maker caste, infused them with Veil-serums brewed by the Weavers, and let the Veil complete the mutation. These became the ogres — 600 kg bear-like titans with the mind of a six- or eight-year-old who never learned speech or society. Tools, not people. No females. The machine opened, the ogre marched to war.

New ogres still appear the same way today. The machines were never destroyed — only hidden.

Thus the three races were born from one blood and one sin.

The Breath was given to Man as restraint (passive, triggered only by high piety and Veilspawn).  
The Veil was given to Woman as blade (active magic, corruption risk).  

The wound still breathes.
